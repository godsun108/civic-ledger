# Civic Ledger

An independent public-information engine that makes public records understandable, traceable, and accessible, starting with the United States and designed for additional jurisdictions.

## Mission
For each public event: What happened? Who acted? What primary record supports it? What changed? What happens next? What remains uncertain?

## Boundaries
Civic Ledger ingests and structures evidence; it is not a court, official government service, legal adviser, or publisher of political persuasion. It supplies versioned evidence packets to [CME](https://github.com/godsun108/civic-media-engine). [VHE](https://github.com/godsun108/virtual-human-engine) may present approved educational explainers.

## Record safeguards
Record public/restricted/sealed/unavailable status; never attempt to circumvent access restrictions. Preserve original links, retrieval timestamps, jurisdiction, source attribution, document revisions, uncertainty, and correction history. Protect personal information even where records are public.

## First milestone
Create source registry, document metadata, claim/evidence links, versioned evidence packet exports, and a source-first “Show Me the Record” view.
